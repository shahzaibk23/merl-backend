Who Are We?

Meet the Entire Team

The Micro Electronics Research Lab is dedicated to empowering students and fostering their professional growth in the field of microelectronics. We provide a comprehensive platform for undergraduate students, offering education and training in Computer Architecture and the interface between hardware and software using the Open Source RISC-V ISA. Our goal is to bridge the gap between theoretical knowledge and practical application, enabling students to excel in industry-standard research projects and prepare for advanced academic pursuits.

Vision

To position Pakistan as a recognized global leader in the microelectronics industry.

Mission

To establish MERL chapters in universities across Pakistan, facilitating research and development on RISC-V based ASICs, FPGAs, and SoCs.

Philosophy

MERL is committed to leading microelectronics research and development in Pakistan. We strive to inspire and motivate our team by demonstrating the highest standards of personal and professional excellence. Our approach combines cutting-edge engineering with a strong emphasis on mentorship and personal development.

Our Journey

The Micro Electronics Research Lab was founded with a visionary goal to introduce and establish a robust semiconductor ecosystem in Pakistan. This ambitious journey began with the strong support of the late Dr. Zahir Ali Syed, who generously provided a small lab at UIT University. His belief in our mission laid the foundation for MERL, enabling us to start with limited resources but unlimited passion.

Late

Dr. Zahir Ali Syed

Mentor, Dreamer and Pillar of MERL

One of our most significant milestones was the successful tape-out of Pakistan's first microprocessors through the Open Source MPW Shuttle program, sponsored by Google. This achievement marked a major step forward and showcased our ability to compete on a global scale. The success of this project was celebrated with the esteemed presence of Dr. Arif Alvi, the President of Pakistan, who honored us at the chip celebration ceremony. This event was a testament to the hard work and dedication of our team and the potential of Pakistan in the field of microelectronics.

As we look to the future, MERL remains committed to continuous growth and innovation. Our ongoing projects push the boundaries of what is possible in microelectronics, and we are dedicated to mentoring the next generation of engineers and researchers. Our ultimate goal is to position Pakistan as a recognized global leader in the microelectronics industry.

Empowering Innovation

At MERL, students are encouraged to explore their creativity and propose their own ideas. We provide the necessary resources and mentorship to help turn their innovative concepts into reality. By nurturing their potential and guiding them through complex projects, we aim to uplift students to achieve PhD-level expertise and beyond.
